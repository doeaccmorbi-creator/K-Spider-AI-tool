📊 About This Tool

The KSpider4Kreation Ultimate Trading Terminal is a professional-grade, free-to-use, multi-asset market analytics dashboard. Powered by TradingView's industry-leading charting engine, it delivers real-time price data, candlestick charts, and interactive technical indicators across Stocks, Cryptocurrencies, Forex, and Global Indices — all in one unified workspace.

Key assets tracked include NSE/BSE indices (NIFTY, SENSEX, BANKNIFTY), top cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL), major US indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ), and currency pairs (USDINR, EUR/USD). Designed for traders, investors, analysts, and finance content creators who need powerful market data without a subscription fee.

📘 User Guide

  • 🔹 Switch Assets: Click the symbol name in the Advanced Chart panel and type any symbol (e.g. RELIANCE, AAPL, GOLD) to load its chart.
  • 🔹 Change Timeframe: Use the timeframe toolbar (1m, 5m, 15m, 1H, 1D, 1W) at the top of the chart to switch between intervals.
  • 🔹 Add Indicators: Click the "Indicators" button on the chart toolbar to add RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, EMA, VWAP, and 100+ more.
  • 🔹 Drawing Tools: Use the left toolbar to draw trendlines, Fibonacci retracement levels, channels, and support/resistance zones.
  • 🔹 Multi-Language: Select your preferred language from the top-right dropdown. All UI labels will translate automatically via the integrated MyMemory API.
  • 🔹 Share: Use the green "Share" button to copy viral WhatsApp messages and spread this tool with your community.
